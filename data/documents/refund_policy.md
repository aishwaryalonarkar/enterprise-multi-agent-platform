# Refund Policy

## Eligibility

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of the original purchase.

Refund requests submitted after 30 days require manual review.

Refunds are not automatically approved for fraudulent transactions.

## Premium Customers

Premium customers may receive expedited refund processing.

## Validation

All refund decisions must be validated against the corresponding transaction record.

A transaction marked as refunded must have a corresponding refund record.

## Processing

Standard refund requests are normally processed within 5 business days.
